About the game

Stygian Dungeon: Crypts of Chaos is a real-time, first-person dungeon crawler in the classic tradition. Lead four champions through thirteen levels of crypts, learn spells from scrolls, keep your torches burning and face the Lich King Vorthak.

Fight rats, spiders, mummies and the restless dead, solve lock-and-key puzzles, and keep your party fed and alive. It is a single-player game for Windows PC, played with the mouse and keyboard.

The story

For three centuries, the realm of Almaria knew peace under the wardens of the Silver Flame. Then, beneath the peaks of Mount Karrak, the undying sorcerer Vorthak, the Lich King, woke the ley lines of raw Chaos and spread a blight of shadow over the land.

Every host of royal knights sent into the crypt was consumed by darkness. Now four legendary heroes have come to the door. Their quest: break the runic wards, descend, and defeat Vorthak before the eclipse.

Lead four champions into the crypt

Choose four champions from a roster of twelve and set them in your front and rear ranks. Each trains in four skills: Fighter, Ninja, Priest and Wizard, and grows stronger in the ones you use, from Novice to Master. Put steel in front and sorcery behind, and keep everyone alive: the rear rank cannot reach past the front, so from the back you shoot, throw or cast.

Cast spells from ancient scrolls

Spells are found, not handed out. Scrolls hidden in the crypts teach them to any champion with the skill and rank to read them, and there are fourteen in all, from Fireball and Lightning Bolt to Greater Healing and Inferno. Every spell costs mana and needs time to recover, so choose your moment. The dead do not wait.

Survive the dark

Your party gets hungry and thirsty, and torches burn out. Find food, a waterskin and every torch you can carry, because the dark is patient. Let the party sleep to recover faster, but food and water run down faster too, and any attack wakes everyone. When a champion falls, an Altar of Reincarnation can bring them back.

Descend thirteen levels

Thirteen levels of crypts lead down beneath Mount Karrak, each with its own layout, creatures and secrets, and each opened by a painted cut scene. Press M for the automap to see everywhere your party has been. At the very bottom, on a throne of bone, the Lich King Vorthak is waiting.

Unlock the secrets

The crypts are full of locked doors, hidden walls, pressure plates and traps. Find the bronze, silver and gold keys, pull the levers, mind the webs and trap spouts, and use teleporters to reach places the stairs never will. Story scrolls left by earlier adventurers hint at what lies below.

Face the restless dead

Combat is real time. Click a champion’s weapon to strike, then wait out the swing: stamina and cooldowns decide who lives. There are more than twenty weapons, from swords, axes and maces to bows and throwing knives, and more than thirty pieces of armour to find. Rats, spiders, bats, skeletons, zombies and mummies are only the beginning.
