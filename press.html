One-line description

Race rival farmers in the silliest tractor race around. Dodge chickens, jump hay bales and outrun angry bulls!

Description

Hillbilly Racing is a silly first-person tractor race across the farm! Dodge chickens, cows and angry bulls, hit hay bale ramps, plough through barn shortcuts and drink turbo fuel for a boost. Race by night, in split screen or online with friends, and unlock ridiculous hats!

Key features

  • Five farms, nine races and a Farm Cup
  • Hay bale ramps, barn shortcuts, angry bulls and a race by night
  • 2 player split screen, and online races with friends
  • Turbo fuel for a boost, and chickens, cows and bulls to dodge
  • Unlockable hats. So many hats.
  • Controller support and 32 achievements

One-line description

Lead four champions into a real-time, grid-based dungeon crawler. Wield steel and sorcery, and defeat the Lich King before the eclipse.

Description

From his throne beneath Mount Karrak, the Lich King Vorthak has cast a blight over the realm of Almaria. Choose four champions from twelve and descend through thirteen levels of crypts to stop him before the eclipse.

Fight rats, spiders, mummies and the restless dead in real-time combat, learn spells from ancient scrolls, solve lock-and-key puzzles and keep your party fed and alive. Steel, sorcery and nerve are all that stand between Almaria and eternal darkness.

Key features

  • Choose four champions from twelve
  • Thirteen levels of crypts, with real-time, grid-based combat
  • Learn spells from ancient scrolls: fireballs, lightning, healing and more
  • Lock-and-key puzzles, hidden doors and traps
  • Keep your party fed, watered and lit: food, water and torches run out
  • An automap that fills in as you explore, and 19 achievements

The studio

About Crimson Viking Games

Crimson Viking Games is an independent game studio making silly and spooky games for PC. Its first releases are Hillbilly Racing, a first-person tractor race, and Stygian Dungeon: Crypts of Chaos, a real-time dungeon crawler. Both are out now on the Epic Games Store.
