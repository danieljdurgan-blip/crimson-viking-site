Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how Crimson Viking Games ("we", "us") and our games capture, use, share and retain information. It covers our games, Hillbilly Racing and Stygian Dungeon: Crypts of Chaos, and this website.

The short version: our games keep your settings and progress on your own PC. When you play through Steam or the Epic Games Store, that store signs you in and records your achievements, and in online games other players see your player name. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking, and we never sell data.

Who we are

Crimson Viking Games is an independent game studio. For anything about this policy or your information, email crimsonvikinggames@gmail.com.

1. How data is captured

On your own PC. Our games save these things on your computer so they're there next time you play. They aren't sent to us.

  • Your settings, such as volume, controls, brightness and graphics quality.
  • Your progress and saved games, such as races won, unlocked items (for example hats in Hillbilly Racing) and achievement progress.
  • The player name you type in for multiplayer games.
  • Technical log files: Stygian Dungeon writes eos.log (whether signing in to Epic and unlocking achievements worked) and crash.log with crash report files (technical details if the game stops unexpectedly). These contain no personal details.

When you play online. Hillbilly Racing lets you race other people:

  • Other players' games receive your player name, your character's appearance (such as your hat and shirt) and what you do in the race.
  • On a local network (LAN), the games connect directly between computers, so the host's computer receives your computer's network (IP) address, as with any online connection.

Through Steam. If you play a Steam copy, Valve handles sign-in, lobbies, invites, friend lists, achievements and connections between players, using your Steam account (such as your Steam name and Steam ID).

Through the Epic Games Store. If you start a game from the Epic Games Launcher, the game signs you in to Epic Online Services with the launcher's one-time sign-in code. With the permission you give in Epic's sign-in prompt, the game receives your Epic account ID and display name, and Epic creates an ID for you in our game. When you unlock an achievement, the game tells Epic which one. Hillbilly Racing's online play also uses your friends list (to send invites) and your network address (to connect players). Epic's software also receives the technical details it needs to provide these services, such as your IP address, operating system and software versions.

Purchases. The store you buy from handles your purchase. We never see your payment details. Stores send us sales reports that don't identify you personally.

This website and email. This website has no cookies, forms, analytics or tracking. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may briefly record technical details such as your IP address in its server logs. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

What we never capture: your real name (unless you email us), postal address, payment details, contacts, precise location, camera or microphone, advertising IDs, or analytics about how you play.

2. How data is used

  • Settings, progress and player name: to keep your game as you left it and show your name to the people you race.
  • Network address: only to connect players in an online game, while you are playing.
  • Steam or Epic account ID, display name and in-game ID: only to sign you in, so your achievements, invites and online games work with your own account.
  • Achievement unlocks: to record achievements on your Steam or Epic account.
  • Friends list: only to let you invite friends to a race. We don't store it.
  • Log files: to help fix problems, and only if you choose to send them to us.
  • Emails: only to reply to you.

We don't use any information for advertising, marketing or profiling, and we make no automated decisions about you.

3. How data is shared

  • Other players in your online game see your player name, your character's appearance and what you do in the game. The host of a LAN game receives your network address.
  • Valve (Steam) handles the information described above under the Steam Privacy Policy.
  • Epic Games handles the information described above under the Epic Games Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub hosts this website and handles its server logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We don't sell, rent or share anyone's information with anyone else: no advertisers, analytics companies or data brokers. We would only disclose information if the law required it.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Signing in to Steam or Epic is handled by those stores, which apply their own protections for younger players. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Connections to Steam and Epic's services are encrypted. Files on your PC are protected by your Windows user account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR or Californian law), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to how it is used, and to complain to your data protection authority. We usually hold no personal information beyond emails you send us, but you can always ask at crimsonvikinggames@gmail.com, and we aim to reply within 30 days. For information held by Steam or Epic Games, you can also use their own privacy settings and pages above.

Changes to this policy

If our games start handling information differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
